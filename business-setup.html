Business setup pathways in Saudi Arabia

The appropriate incorporation route depends on your current corporate status, intended commercial activity, operational scope, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Foreign company branch setup

For established international enterprises expanding their commercial footprint into Saudi Arabia under an authorized corporate branch or foreign subsidiary structure.

Who this pathway is intended for

Active foreign companies outside Saudi Arabia that possess documented commercial registration, verified audited financials, and an operational track record in their home jurisdiction.

Purpose and legal identity of a Saudi branch

A branch operates as an official legal extension of the overseas parent company in Saudi Arabia, enabling direct commercial contracting, government bidding, localized invoicing, and hiring under the parent's brand reputation.

Regulatory checks & documentation

Requires obtaining a Foreign Investment License from the Ministry of Investment (MISA), board resolutions authorizing the branch, certified articles of association, and attestation through the Saudi Embassy and Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).

What information to prepare

Valid Certificate of Incorporation from home jurisdiction, last 1–2 years audited balance sheets, list of intended commercial activities, and designated General Manager details.

Note: Branch approval is governed by MISA regulatory criteria and sector guidelines. Certain activities may require specific capital commitments or secondary ministerial approvals.

Starting a business as an expatriate

For expatriate professionals currently residing in Saudi Arabia looking to launch a commercial venture, subject to applicable regulatory and activity requirements.

Who this pathway is intended for

Resident expatriates in the Kingdom who possess valid residency (Iqama), relevant professional background, and a defined commercial concept ready for formal incorporation.

Selecting an appropriate commercial activity

Saudi Arabia utilizes the ISIC4 activity classification system. Activities must be rigorously categorized (consulting, trading, services, tech, etc.) to determine whether 100% foreign ownership applies or specific licensing constraints exist.

Ownership, licensing & capital checks

Evaluating whether your intended activity qualifies under entrepreneurship/investor pathways, confirming minimum statutory capital requirements, and assessing legal structure options (Single-Person LLC vs Standard LLC).

Information needed for initial discussion

Clear description of the proposed business, target customer segment, intended commercial activities, and current Iqama status.
