Required Overseas Documents.

To establish a foreign subsidiary or branch, the overseas corporate parent must supply four core authenticated instruments:

1
Certificate of Commercial Registration: Showing active corporate standing in origin jurisdiction for at least one fiscal year.
2
Articles of Association / Memorandum: Detailing shareholder identity and parent corporate object clauses.
3
Audited Financial Statements: Certified balance sheet and profit & loss for the preceding year from an accredited auditor.
4
Board Resolution & Power of Attorney: Formally resolving to incorporate in KSA and appointing the General Manager.

Document Attestation Protocol

Saudi Arabia is an official member of the Hague Apostille Convention. If your enterprise is incorporated in an Apostille signatory jurisdiction (such as the UK, USA, France, Germany, or UAE), corporate documents require only an official Apostille certificate. Non-signatory jurisdictions require sequential legalization through the local Chamber of Commerce, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the Royal Embassy of Saudi Arabia.
