Foreign Investor Protections & Statutory Environment.

Under the modernized Saudi Investment Law, international enterprises and resident expat founders operate with complete legal certainty, national treatment equality, and robust statutory safeguards.

Complete National Treatment Parity

Foreign investors are granted identical statutory rights, commercial privileges, and regulatory standing as domestic Saudi businesses. Full protection against discriminatory commercial conduct.

Abolition of Local Sponsor Mandates

Total elimination of mandatory local Saudi commercial agents, sponsors, or proxy partners. Incorporate with 100% direct foreign or resident expat equity under official MISA and Ministry of Commerce decrees.

Institutional SAIP IP Protections

Rigorous statutory intellectual property, trademark, and digital patent registration governed by the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) under global international treaty conventions.

Unrestricted Capital Mobility

Unconditional freedom to transfer profits, dividends, royalties, and liquidation proceeds abroad through multi-currency corporate banking, backed by arbitration through the SCCA.

Foreign Enterprise & Branch Expansion.

Engineered for foreign corporations and multinational groups expanding directly into Saudi Arabia to tap national procurement, sovereign tenders, and high-growth enterprise demand.

  • Fast-Track Document Attestation: Complete overseas corporate document screening, apostille routing, and Saudi MOFA attestation coordination.

In-Kingdom Expat Entrepreneurs.

Built for experienced resident expatriates currently in the Kingdom ready to leave employer sponsorship behind and establish their own fully legal, independent commercial enterprise from scratch.

  • Independent Legal Ownership: Clean transition from employment Iqama to licensed corporate shareholder without employer friction or delays.
  • 100% Equity Retention: Incorporate as a single-owner or multi-partner LLC under the modernized Saudi Investment Law with 100% foreign equity.
  • Zero Local Sponsor Dependency: Eliminate outdated "kafeel" arrangements, informal side agreements, and opaque profit-sharing fees.
  • Investor Iqama Status: Secure official General Manager Investor Iqama status for you and executive visas for your founding team and family.
