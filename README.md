# Siraj Ventures — Saudi Market Entry Platform

> A founder-led Saudi market platform for business setup, MISA licensing, executive residency, and operational scale. Built for the Kingdom's next decade.

## Overview
Siraj Ventures provides foreign founders, institutional investors, and international enterprises with direct, on-the-ground intelligence and structured advisory to enter and scale in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

## Design Architecture & System
Completely redesigned with an institutional, editorial aesthetic inspired by **Analytix** and aligned with Saudi Arabia's prestigious sovereign emerald identity:
- **Custom Color Palette**:
  - `#9DD8BA` — Soft Mint (Tint highlights, badge backgrounds, indicator tags)
  - `#75BF85` — Sage Green (Secondary accents, borders, trendline accents)
  - `#42A555` — Vibrant Emerald (Indicator highlights, progress meter fill, play button states)
  - `#1E8047` — Rich Forest Green (Primary action color, main CTA buttons, key links)
  - `#18583B` — Deep Pine (Dark hover states, secondary dark accents)
  - `#103A27` — Deep Forest Navy (Primary corporate dark, featured bento cards, hero card)
  - `#071911` — Onyx Forest (Deepest night dark, top status bar, multi-column footer)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts `DM Sans` paired with `Plus Jakarta Sans`, featuring tight headline tracking and generous body line heights.
- **Layouts**: Asymmetric Bento 50/50 grids, interactive capability switchers, and zero generic AI templates (no floating sparkles or random gradients).
- **Interactions**:
  - **Executive Video Masterclasses**: Privacy-conscious lightbox modal player for curated founder briefings.
  - **Interactive Saudi Readiness Diagnostic**: 6-step diagnostic calculating customized legal pathways, readiness scoring (out of 100), and automated WhatsApp handoff.
  - **Economic Momentum Area Chart**: Interactive SVG trendline with real-time hover tooltips for FDI stock, non-oil GDP, and RHQ indicators.
  - **Advisory Consultation Hub**: Intent-based inquiry routing with direct WhatsApp integration and email fallbacks.
