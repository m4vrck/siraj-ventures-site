The four-stage business setup journey

Setting up in Saudi Arabia is a logical sequence of regulatory checkpoints. We replace ambiguity with transparent stages, clearly identifying what is required at each milestone.

Transparent progression from initial scoping to operational launch.

Rather than generic promises, business setup requires diligent coordination between statutory requirements, ministerial approvals, and post-registration obligations. Here is how an engagement with Siraj Ventures unfolds.

Step-by-step milestones to market entry

Each stage builds upon verified prerequisites. Cards stack naturally as you scroll through the execution roadmap.

Stage 01 • Discovery & Evaluation Estimated: 1–2 Weeks

Comprehensive consultation and pathway evaluation

Every engagement begins with an in-depth review of your commercial objectives. We evaluate your existing legal entity structure, your target industry sector, and the specific activities you plan to execute in the Kingdom.

Determining whether a foreign branch or new company formation applies
Cross-referencing commercial activities with the official Saudi ISIC4 activity database
Clarifying statutory foreign equity limits and minimum capital commitments
Establishing a realistic, sector-specific operational schedule
Stage 02 • Structuring & Attestation Estimated: 2–4 Weeks

Preparing and verifying all required corporate documentation

Incomplete or uncertified documentation is the most frequent cause of regulatory delays. In Stage 2, we organize, verify, and format the precise document dossier tailored to your chosen pathway.

Parent company documentation assembly (Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association)
Drafting formal Board Resolutions authorizing Saudi market entry and appointing General Manager
Coordination of certified Arabic translations by approved legal translators
Guiding consular authentication and Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation
Stage 03 • Statutory Approvals & Licensing Estimated: 1–3 Weeks

Navigating licensing through relevant government authorities

Once documentation is verified, applications are submitted through official Saudi ministerial channels. We coordinate filings through the Saudi Business Center (SBC) portal to secure statutory approvals.

Ministry of Investment (MISA) Foreign Investment License issuance
Drafting and notarizing Articles of Association through Ministry of Commerce (MC)
Issuance of official Commercial Registration (CR) Certificate
Enrollment in the Riyadh Chamber of Commerce & Industry
Notice: Statutory issuance timelines depend on governmental review, background checks, and sector classification. Siraj Ventures coordinates filings diligently but does not claim unilateral power over official approvals.
Stage 04 • Post-Incorporation & Readiness Estimated: 2–3 Weeks

Establishing ongoing operational and regulatory compliance

Receiving your Commercial Registration is only the legal foundation. To trade, invoice, and employ personnel in Saudi Arabia, specific operational portals must be configured.

Corporate bank account opening coordination with authorized Saudi commercial banks
Tax registration with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) for corporate income tax & VAT
Enrollment in the Qiwa platform (Ministry of Human Resources) for workforce and labor management
General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) file establishment

Every business journey is unique.

Whether you are preparing a corporate parent dossier or evaluating an expat venture in Riyadh, an initial consultation will clarify the required steps.
