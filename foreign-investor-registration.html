Foreign Investor Registration in Saudi Arabia.

The Ministry of Investment (MISA) issues foreign investment licenses authorizing overseas corporations and qualified international entrepreneurs to operate with 100% foreign equity across Saudi Arabia.

Request MISA Licensing Review

MISA Investment License Categories.

MISA issues licenses based on the international standard ISIC4 economic classifications. Choosing the accurate activity scope is critical, as modifying commercial activities post-incorporation requires formal ministerial amendments.

1. Services & Consulting License

Covers technical, engineering, software development, management consulting, and marketing activities. No mandatory statutory capital threshold for standard consulting.

2. Commercial Trading License

Enables wholesale and retail import, storage, and direct distribution. Available with 100% foreign ownership with committed capital or in joint venture format.

3. Industrial & Manufacturing License

Permits factory establishment, raw material processing, and equipment fabrication. Qualifies for MODON industrial land and customs duty exemptions on plant machinery.

4. Real Estate Development License

Allows international developers to acquire, develop, and construct major residential, commercial, and mixed-use hospitality assets across designated investment zones.

MISA Checklist for Foreign Entities

  • ✓ Certificate of Incorporation in parent country
  • ✓ Articles of Association / Operating Agreement
  • ✓ Audited Financial Statements for prior 12 months
  • ✓ Board Resolution approving Saudi incorporation
  • ✓ Appointed General Manager identification & CV
  • ✓ Hague Apostille or Saudi Embassy attestation
