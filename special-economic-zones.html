Special Economic Zones (SEZ) in Saudi Arabia.

Benefit from 5% corporate income tax for up to 20 years, 0% customs duties on machinery, and 0% withholding tax on profit repatriation within designated industrial, logistics, and digital zones.

Explore SEZ Locations

King Abdullah Economic City (KAEC) SEZ.

Strategic maritime logistics and advanced manufacturing hub located on the Red Sea coast, adjacent to King Abdullah Port.

Explore KAEC

Jazan Special Economic Zone.

Heavy industrial, metals processing, and energy hub providing raw material access and connectivity to East African markets.

Explore Jazan

Ras Al-Khair Special Economic Zone.

Maritime industries, offshore vessel fabrication, and shipbuilding center in the Arabian Gulf adjacent to major offshore energy fields.
