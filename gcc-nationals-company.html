Company Registration for GCC Nationals in Saudi Arabia.

Citizens and enterprises from the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman benefit from direct national economic treatment under the Gulf Cooperation Council economic treaty, registering without MISA foreign licensing.

Direct Ministry of Commerce Registration.

Unlike other international investors who must first secure an investment license from MISA, GCC nationals and GCC-owned corporations can establish Limited Liability Companies (LLCs) or sole proprietorships directly with the Ministry of Commerce (MC).

Key GCC Advantages in Saudi Arabia:

  • Exemption from MISA Licensing: Saves significant upfront government fees and avoids foreign licensing tiers.
  • Zakat Tax Status: GCC-owned entities are subject to standard 2.5% Zakat rather than the 20% foreign corporate income tax.
  • Commercial Property Rights: Full legal entitlement to acquire real estate for business operations across the Kingdom.
  • Fast-Track Registration: Commercial Registrations (CR) are issued within 5 to 7 business days.
