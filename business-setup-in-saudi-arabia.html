Foreign Investor Rights Under the Updated Saudi Investment Law.

Under Vision 2030 and the modernized statutory investment framework, Saudi Arabia guarantees international investors national treatment, absolute legal certainty, and unhindered capital mobility.

100% Foreign Shareholding.

Foreign corporations and individual investors can incorporate commercial, industrial, healthcare, logistics, and technical consulting entities with 100% foreign equity across the Kingdom.

Full Capital & Profit Repatriation.

Absolute statutory protection to transfer 100% of dividends, royalty payments, and liquidation proceeds back to overseas corporate parent accounts with zero domestic currency restrictions.

Removal of Local Sponsorship.

Direct independent corporate ownership. The historical requirement for local Saudi nominee partners or silent sponsor agreements has been completely abolished for foreign enterprises.

National Treatment Parity.

Foreign investors enjoy identical protections as Saudi domestic firms, including government tender eligibility, institutional industrial land allocations, and access to commercial dispute courts.

End-to-End Statutory Incorporation Roadmap (4 Steps).

Siraj Ventures structures and accelerates every statutory touchpoint across ministerial portals, legal notaries, and tier-one banking institutions.

1

Statutory Deliverable: Ministry of Investment (MISA) Foreign Investment License.
We conduct comprehensive pre-screening of your commercial activities under the official ISIC4 code classification. Our team oversees consular legalization of your parent company's Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, audited balance sheets, and board resolutions through the Saudi Embassy and Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA). Once attested, we lodge the investment dossier with MISA for expedited digital license issuance.

2

Statutory Deliverable: Ministry of Commerce Commercial Registration (Sijil Tijari).
We draft custom bilingual (Arabic-English) Articles of Association compliant with the Saudi Companies Law, codifying corporate governance, share capital, voting thresholds, and General Manager executive authorities. Following digital notarization via the Ministry of Justice, the Commercial Registration (CR) is electronically issued via the Saudi Business Center (SBC) single-window gateway.

3

Statutory Deliverable: Full Ministerial Compliance Registry & Tax Credentials.
Post-CR activation across key national regulatory portals: (1) Wasel National Address registration for official corporate geolocation, (2) Riyadh Chamber of Commerce certificate attestation, (3) Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) registration for Corporate Income Tax and VAT, and (4) Qiwa and General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) file openings for labor and employment compliance.

4

Statutory Deliverable: Operational Commercial Facility & Active Corporate IBAN.
We expedite executive work authorization, medical fitness clearance, and resident identity (Iqama) for the designated General Manager. In parallel, we secure the municipal operational permit through the Balady portal for your office premises. Finally, our senior banking liaisons prepare institutional AML/KYC files and coordinate onboarding with premier Saudi banks (Al Rajhi, Saudi National Bank, Riyad Bank, SAB) to activate corporate multi-currency accounts.

Saudi Tax Architecture, ZATCA Phase 2 & ISIC4 Classification.

Understanding post-incorporation tax liabilities, e-invoicing integrations, and activity code alignments is vital for sustained corporate compliance.

Corporate Income Tax (CIT).

A competitive flat 20% tax rate applies solely to the foreign shareholder's net adjusted profits. Saudi Arabia levies 0% personal income tax on salaries and 0% local capital gains tax on qualifying share sales.

ZATCA Fatoora Phase 2.

Mandatory integration between your ERP or billing platform and ZATCA via cryptographic APIs. Requires cryptographic stamp generation, UUIDs, and real-time XML transmission for all B2B and B2C sales.

Withholding Tax (WHT).

Applies to non-resident service payments: 5% on dividends, 5% on loan interest, 15% on royalties, and 15% to 20% on management or technical fees, often mitigated by Double Tax Treaties.

ISIC4 Activity Classification.

Strict harmonization of commercial activities under the UN ISIC4 hierarchy. Siraj Ventures verifies that your MISA license, Articles of Association, CR, and Balady municipal permit align without discrepancy.

For standard professional services, technical consulting, engineering, and IT companies, there is no statutory minimum share capital mandated by MISA. A nominal capital of SAR 10,000 to SAR 25,000 is standard in the Articles of Association. Certain regulated sectors require specialized capital commitments: for instance, trading/retail entities requiring 100% foreign ownership must commit SAR 30 million over three years or operate under standard foreign wholesale licenses requiring SAR 500,000 capital.

These documents must be attested by the Saudi Embassy in the origin country and legalized by the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).
